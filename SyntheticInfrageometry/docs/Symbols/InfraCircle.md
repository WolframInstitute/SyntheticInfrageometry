## Usage

`InfraCircle[{cycle}]` is the unary form; InfraCircle[{cycle1, ..., cyclek}] is the multi-realisation form.

## Details & Options

Find* returns one wrapper carrying the requested realisations.

Scene-language constructor InfraCircle[center, radius] is used inside InfraScene.

The multi form is consumed by InfraSceneHighlight (sequential edges with auto-closure).
