## Usage

`InfraAngle[graph, {q1, p, q2}]` returns the angle at p in radians.

## Details & Options

Option Method ("Arclength" (default; boundary arclength outside the ball, divided by the radius), "Alexandrov", {"Alexandrov", "Curvature" -> k}).
