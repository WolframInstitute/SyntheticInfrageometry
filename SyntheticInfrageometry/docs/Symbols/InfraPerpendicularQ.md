## Usage

`InfraPerpendicularQ[graph, l1, l2]` tests whether two lines are perpendicular at every common vertex (False on empty intersection); inside InfraScene the graph-free InfraPerpendicularQ[l1, l2] asserts the same.

## Details & Options

Options Method ("Projection" (default), "Coordinate", "Arclength", "Alexandrov"; per-method sub-options live inside the spec), "Radius".
