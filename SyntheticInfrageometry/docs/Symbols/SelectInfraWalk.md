## Usage

`SelectInfraWalk[graph, walks]` draws a walk from a bundle -- vertex lists or walk graphs, cycle graphs selecting as closed walks -- treated as a metric space.

## Details & Options

Options "From", "Distance", "Metric", "MaxCliques", "Cyclic".
