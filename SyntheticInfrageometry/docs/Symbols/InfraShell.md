## Usage

`InfraShell[{set}]` is the unary form (one metric shell vertex set); InfraShell[{set1, ..., setk}] is the multi-realisation form.

## Details & Options

Find* returns one wrapper carrying the requested realisations.

Scene-language constructor InfraShell[center, radius] is used inside InfraScene.

The multi form is consumed by InfraSceneHighlight (induced-subgraph semantics).
