## Usage

`InfraVolume[g, s]` is the volume of s (a bare vertex list or any Infra* object).

## Details & Options

Options:

| Option | Values |
|---|---|
| `"Volume"` | "Hausdorff" (default; \|s\| minus its boundary), "Counting", "Boundary" |
| `Method` | "Combinatorial" (default), {"Alexandrov", "Radius" -> r} |
