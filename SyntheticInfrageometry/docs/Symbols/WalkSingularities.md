## Usage

`WalkSingularities[walk]` gives the singularities of a walk (a vertex list or a walk graph; a cycle graph is read on its cyclic core) as parameter data: "SelfIntersections" (position groups sharing a vertex), "SelfTangencies" (oriented interval groups sharing an arc), "Cusps" (mirrored blocks).
