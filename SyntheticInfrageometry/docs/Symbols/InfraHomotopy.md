## Usage

`InfraHomotopy[{chain}]` is the unary form: one homotopy chain {p0, p1, ..., pk} of intermediate walks produced by k elementary moves from p0 to pk.

`InfraHomotopy[{chain1, ..., chaink}]` is the multi-realisation form.

## Details & Options

Find* finders return one InfraHomotopy wrapper carrying the requested chains.
